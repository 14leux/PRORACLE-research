# Code of Conduct

PRORACLE is a public research and software project. We expect people to treat one another with respect and to discuss evidence without personal attacks.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission. Criticism of ideas, methods, code, and results is welcome when it stays focused on the work.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately by email to **security@sultangreen.com** with "Conduct" in the subject line. Do not use a public issue for the report itself.

This policy applies in project issues, discussions, pull requests, and project spaces where a contributor represents PRORACLE.
