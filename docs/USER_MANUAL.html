The Rule Lab: how to play with it

A pretend ticket. Several players guess the same matches before kickoff. When the matches end, we count who was right. You invent the players.

The idea in four pictures

1. Matches

A list of games, 10 to 20. Today's list comes by itself. You can also send a PDF.

2. Everyone picks

Home win, draw or away win. Each player picks all the same games.

3. Locked

Picks are frozen before kickoff. Nobody can change them after the match.

4. Score

When games end, we count each player's right guesses. That is the scoreboard.

It is a report card, not betting advice. It never says what to stake or what you would win.

Meet the players

A

The model

Picks whatever the rating model thinks is most likely. Never looks at odds. You cannot change it.

C

The draw lover

Follows A, except the five games where a draw looks likeliest. There it picks draw. Never looks at odds.

Your players: up to five rules

Each rule you add becomes its own player. Rule 3 is called B3. It follows A, except where your rule says to change a pick.

B1B2B3B4B5five slots at once

One match, everyone's pick. This is what /today shows you (example):

MatchAB1 (2 draws)B2 (1 upset)C
Lions v Tigershomehomeaway reverseddraw forced
Reds v Blueshomedraw forcedhomehome

A rule is only different from A on the few picks it changes. That is where we find out if the rule helps.

Play along: your first 10 minutes

Open your bot in Telegram. Type exactly what is in the green bubbles. Grey bubbles show what the bot says back (yours will look a little different).

  1. See what you can do

    /help
    BotWhat you can do: ... /rule, /retire, YES, NO, /rules, /ticket, /scoreboard ...

    The new buttons (like /retire) show up in Telegram's menu after your first message following today's update.

  2. Invent your first player

    /rule 5 draws
    BotProposed rule v1 as arm B1: force the 5 highest draw scores to DRAW Nothing changes until you reply YES (it waits 60 minutes). Reply NO to cancel. 0 of 5 rule slots are in use.
    YES
    BotRule v1 is now active as arm B1: force the 5 highest draw scores to DRAW Active rules: v1 (1 of 5). It applies to tickets frozen from now on.

    The bot never starts a rule without your YES. If you do not answer for an hour, the proposal expires.

  3. Add rivals, one change at a time

    /rule 2 draws
    YES
    /rule 3 upsets by margin
    YES
    /rule 3 draws; only Premier League
    YES

    You now have B1 (5 draws), B2 (2 draws), B3 (3 upsets), B4 (3 draws in one league). Good experiments change one thing: 5 draws against 2 draws tells you something. Use the rule builder below to write the words for you.

  4. Check your players

    /rules
    BotActive rules (4 of 5; each is its own arm B on the same fixtures as A and C): - B1 (rule v1): force the 5 highest draw scores to DRAW - B2 (rule v2): force the 2 highest draw scores to DRAW - B3 (rule v3): reverse 3 picks chosen by smallest model margin - B4 (rule v4): force the 3 highest draw scores to DRAW; only leagues: Premier League

  5. Send a PDF of fixtures

    Attach the PDF or a screenshot. The bot reads it (one of your 10 reads for the day), then replies twice.

    Bot, message 1Read 41 row(s); using 20. Matched 10, unmatched 10 ... Computing arms A, C and B1, B2, B3, B4 (one per active rule) now; the frozen picks follow in this chat.
    Bot, message 2 (a minute later)Frozen before kickoff: 10 fixture(s), arms A, B1, B2, B3, B4, C. - Home 1 v Away 1: A home | B1 draw (forced) | B2 home | B3 away (reversed) | B4 home | C draw (forced) ... Arm B is one pick set per active rule (B3 is rule v3): - rule v1: 5 of 5 requested adjustments applied on 10 fixture(s) in scope - rule v4: 3 of 3 requested adjustments applied on 4 fixture(s) in scope

    Picks are locked at that moment, before kickoff. Games already kicked off are left out and listed.

  6. Look at today's tickets

    /today
    BotSimulated ticket, 20 fixtures: - Lions v Tigers: A home | B1 draw (forced) | B2 home | C draw (forced) ... Screenshot ticket, 10 fixtures: ...

  7. Come back later and read the report card

    /scoreboard
    Bot (example numbers only)Hit rates on settled picks of complete tickets (a calibration report card, not evidence of an edge). The ± is a rough 95% margin for one hit rate: a gap smaller than it is most likely noise. Simulated tickets: - A (rating model): 22 of 40 right (55.0%, ±15 pts) over 3 ticket(s) - B1 (rule v1): 21 of 40 right (52.5%, ±15 pts) over 3 ticket(s) Same fixtures: A 22 right, C 20 right Where it differs from A by design: 9 settled, it was right 3 times and A 4 - C (draw-score-v1): 20 of 40 right (50.0%, ±15 pts) over 3 ticket(s) Same fixtures: A 22 right Where it differs from A by design: 9 settled, it was right 3 times and A 4

    Results come after the matches end and a scheduled run records them (see When). Until then it says "No settled ticket picks yet."

  8. Retire a player that is not working

    /retire 2
    BotStop rule v2 (arm B2): force the 2 highest draw scores to DRAW? It would no longer be used on tickets frozen from now on; its earlier picks stay and are still scored. Reply YES to stop it (it waits 60 minutes) or NO to keep it.
    YES
    BotRule v2 is retired (arm B2). Its earlier picks stay and are still scored. Active rules: v1, v3, v4.

    The slot is free again. Start a new rule and it gets the next version number (v5), so a new name and a fresh count from zero.

When things happen

By itself, every day

The daily ticket (from games in the next 24 hours, 10 to 20 of them), locked before kickoff, with whatever rules are active at that moment. Then results get recorded and scored.

Only when you act

PDF tickets, new rules, stopping rules, looking at things. Nothing else changes while you are away. On quiet days the data still grows.

Times drift. GitHub sometimes starts a scheduled run late, by minutes or even an hour. The times are 05:37 and 17:37 UTC.
Try it tonight: add your rules now. Tomorrow after 08:37, /today shows a daily ticket with B1, B2 ... next to A and C. The first scores appear after those games finish and a later run records them.

A rule you add late in the day first appears on the next day's daily ticket, because the ticket is written once and never edited. A PDF ticket uses your rules straight away.

Who is winning (same as /scoreboard)
select ticket_kind, arm, arm_version, tickets, picks_settled, picks_right,
       round(100.0 * picks_right / picks_settled, 1) as pct_right,
       a_right_same_fixtures, c_right_same_fixtures,
       adjusted_settled, adjusted_right, a_right_on_adjusted
from proracle.intake_scoreboard
order by ticket_kind, arm, arm_version;
Empty until games have finished. adjusted_* are the picks where a rule or C differs from A on purpose.
Today's picks, every player side by side
select f.kickoff_at, f.home_team, f.away_team,
       case when p.arm = 'B' then 'B' || replace(p.arm_version, 'rule-v', '') else p.arm end as player,
       p.pick, p.adjustment, p.forced
from proracle.ticket_arm_picks p
join proracle.tickets t on t.id = p.ticket_id
join proracle.fixtures f on f.id = p.fixture_id
where t.ticket_date = current_date
order by f.kickoff_at, f.home_team, player;
One row per player per match. adjustment says FORCED_DRAW or REVERSED when a rule changed A's pick.
Finished matches: who was right
select f.kickoff_at, f.home_team, f.away_team, r.actual_outcome as result,
       case when p.arm = 'B' then 'B' || replace(p.arm_version, 'rule-v', '') else p.arm end as player,
       p.pick, p.pick = r.actual_outcome as right_pick
from proracle.ticket_arm_picks p
join proracle.fixtures f on f.id = p.fixture_id
join proracle.results r on r.fixture_id = p.fixture_id and r.status = 'FINAL'
  and not exists (select 1 from proracle.results s where s.supersedes_result_id = r.id)
order by f.kickoff_at desc, f.home_team, player
limit 200;
The raw material behind the scoreboard. Sort or filter it to spot patterns (for example only the rows where player starts with B).

What the bot has learned about names
select r.observed_at, r.home_team as typed_home, f.home_team as stored_home,
       r.away_team as typed_away, f.away_team as stored_away
from proracle.intake_ticket_rows r
join proracle.fixtures f on f.id = r.matched_fixture_id
where r.home_team <> f.home_team or r.away_team <> f.away_team
order by r.observed_at desc;
Each row is a spelling from a PDF that the bot tied to a stored name. These pairs are its memory.

How it learns

Three different things, so you know which one to watch.

memory

Team names: yes

Every PDF row it matched teaches it a spelling. Next time that spelling works at once.

model

Arm A: on its own

The rating model keeps reading finished results by itself. You do not train it, and rules do not change it.

rules

Rules: you learn

The system never edits a rule. It shows you the scores. You decide: keep it, retire it, try another.

The name memory, in three pictures

Experiment: send the same PDF twice (each send uses one of the day's 10 reads). Compare the two replies. The second should list fewer "check these" matches and say how many it remembered. The audit-log query shows remembered= as a number.

What it cannot learn yet

Names that share no whole word with the stored name never match, for example Hearts, RB Salzburg and Olympiakos from your first PDF. The bot does not guess. An alias command would let you teach it those by hand; we agreed to decide that after you send about 10 PDFs and we can see which names keep failing. There is also no "forget" button yet: if you ever see a wrong pair in the "what the bot has learned" query, tell me.

Four habits

1. Compare a rule with A on the same fixtures. The scoreboard prints that for you.
2. Look at "where it differs from A". That is the only place a rule can win or lose.
3. Count a fixture once. The same PDF sent twice makes two tickets with the same games.
4. On a short 10-game ticket, C forces 5 draws, which is half its picks. Read C with that in mind.

Plain truth: a few days of results cannot separate rules that differ by a few points. Wait for hundreds of settled picks before believing a gap. The scoreboard is a report card, not evidence of an edge, and not advice to stake money.

Rule words

N draws (1 to 10)
N upsets by margin or by odds
only League, League or skip League (not both)

Limits

5 rules at once. 20 fixtures per PDF ticket. 10 PDF reads per UTC day. Files up to 10 MB. Daily ticket from 10 games, up to 20. Waiting questions expire after 60 minutes.

What the bot will never do

Change the model or arm A. Put odds into A or C. Give stake, bankroll or profit advice. Start or stop a rule without your YES. Answer any chat but yours.
