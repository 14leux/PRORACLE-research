# Data and Report Policy

## What may be committed

- Synthetic examples created for this repository.
- Data and reports that the contributor has clear rights to redistribute.
- Aggregate findings only after checking provider terms, privacy, and whether the original data could be reconstructed.

## What must not be committed

- API keys, bot tokens, database URLs with credentials, private keys, or `.env` files.
- Raw provider responses, paid or restricted datasets, bookmaker prices, or private production exports without written redistribution permission.
- Personal information, private Telegram reports, account identifiers, or screenshots that reveal private systems.
- Small-group or row-level output that could reveal a provider's underlying data or identify a person.

## Attribution and licensing

PRORACLE code is intended to use Apache-2.0. Original project-authored explanatory documents and reports are intended to use CC BY 4.0. These licenses do not grant rights to third-party data, provider APIs, logos, or restricted derived outputs. Follow each source's attribution and usage terms. If rights are unclear, do not include the data; use a synthetic example and ask the provider or a qualified adviser.

Contributors are responsible for documenting the source and permission basis for any non-synthetic material they propose to share. Maintainers may ask for additional evidence or decline material whose rights cannot be established.
